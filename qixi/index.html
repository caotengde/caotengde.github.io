English EN

My dearest Yukari,

There are so many things I hope life gives us, but the one I treasure most is simple: more ordinary days beside you. More mornings, shared meals, small jokes, and quiet evenings when just being together is enough.

You make my world gentler, brighter, and more beautiful. Thank you for choosing me, for understanding me, and for being the person I always want to come home to.

On this Qixi, I want you to know: in every season, every place, and every version of our future, I will keep choosing you.

Happy Qixi, my love.

Always yours,
